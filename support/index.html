Tally Support

Help with playing, levels, Today's Tally and your account.

How Tally works

Play 60-second rounds: tap one of four coloured answers. Five right in a row doubles your points, ten in a row triples them. Questions get harder while you're on a run and easier after a miss. Miss one and Tally shows you a quicker way. Your first round finds your starting level.

Ways to play

Play a round: 60 seconds. Today's Tally: one round a day, the same questions for everyone at your level, with a new one at midnight. Endless: no clock and three lives; it gets harder until your third miss.

Levels

There are eight levels, from Starter to Grandmaster, and each kind of question has its own. Grandmaster has to be earned through play. In the app, open You, then How levels work, to see what each level asks. Levels describe how you're doing in Tally; they aren't a qualification or a school grade.

Your progress

Everything is saved on your iPhone; there's no account. Deleting Tally removes your progress, and a new phone starts fresh.

Reminders and haptics

Turn the daily reminder on or off, and change its time, in You. Haptics can be turned off there too. Tally has no sound.
