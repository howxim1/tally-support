Tally Support

Help with playing, levels, Today's Tally and your account.

How Tally works

Play a set of ten problems. Tally picks problems that are just hard enough, so you get about eight in ten right. Your Tally Rating moves with every set, and your level for each kind of problem rises as you play. A few problems you miss come back for review a few days later. Tally never shows a clock while you play.

Today's Tally

Fifteen problems a day, easy to hard, matched to your level. On Sundays the Big Tally has twenty. A new one starts at midnight on your iPhone. Today's Tally doesn't change your rating. Tap Share on the result to send it as a row of squares.

Levels

There are eight levels, from Starter to Grandmaster. Each kind of problem has its own level. Grandmaster has to be earned through play, so the first test never places you there. In the app, open You, then How levels work, to see what each level asks. Levels describe how you're doing in Tally; they aren't a qualification or a school grade.

Hints and skipping

Tap Hint for a nudge. A hinted answer counts, but moves your rating less. Skip shows the answer and moves on; the problem comes back for review.

Keeping your progress on a new phone

Open You and choose Sign in with Apple. Your progress is saved and appears when you sign in with the same Apple Account on another phone. Signing in is optional; without it, your progress stays only on this iPhone.

Deleting your account

Open You and choose Delete account. This removes your saved progress and your sign-in, and Tally on that phone starts fresh.

Reminders and haptics

Turn the daily reminder on or off, and change its time, in You. Haptics can be turned off there too. Tally has no sound.
