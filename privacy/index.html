Tally Privacy Policy

Effective for Tally 1.0, updated 1 October 2026

Tally is a mental maths game that works offline. You can play without an account, and nothing about your play leaves your iPhone unless you choose to sign in. Tally has no advertising, no tracking and no third-party analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

On your iPhone

Tally keeps your answers, levels, rating, Today's Tally results and settings on your iPhone. A small set of play statistics (for example how many sets you have finished) also stays on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It lets you keep your progress on a new phone. Tally offers Sign in with Apple only. When you sign in:

  • Our sign-in provider, Clerk, receives your Apple sign-in and your email address, which can be Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide your email. Clerk gives your account a user ID.
  • Your progress (your answers, levels, rating and history) is saved with that user ID to our database, so the same progress appears when you sign in on another phone. The database is hosted by Neon and reached through our server on Vercel.

We use this information only to run your account and keep your progress. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or tracking, and we do not combine it with data from other apps or websites.

Reminders

The daily reminder is optional and off unless you turn it on. It is scheduled on your iPhone and does not involve our server.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open You and choose Delete account. This removes your saved progress from our database and your account from Clerk, and Tally on that phone starts fresh. Without an account, deleting Tally from your iPhone removes everything it stored.

Children

Tally is suitable for all ages and works fully without an account. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children; a child can play without signing in.

Changes

If Tally's handling of data changes, this page will be updated before the change reaches the app, with a new date above.
